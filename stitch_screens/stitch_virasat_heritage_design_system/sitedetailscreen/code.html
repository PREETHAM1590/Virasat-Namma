About this Heritage Site

Step into a bygone era where stones whisper tales of a glorious empire. Hampi, the ancient capital of the Vijayanagara Empire, is a surreal landscape of colossal boulders, intricate temple ruins, and sprawling royal pavilions set against the backdrop of the Tungabhadra River.

Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site, its architectural brilliance features monolithic sculptures, ornate pillars, and the iconic stone chariot that continues to captivate historians and travelers alike.
