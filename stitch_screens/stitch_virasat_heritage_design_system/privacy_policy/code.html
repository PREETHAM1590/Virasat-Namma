Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2023

1. Introduction

Welcome to Virasat. As the modern custodians of cultural and natural heritage, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform to explore and preserve historical sites, ecological wonders, and cultural narratives.

By interacting with our services, you entrust us with your digital footprint. We treat this data with the same respect and careful preservation as the physical heritage sites we celebrate.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless, organic experience, we gather certain types of information:

  • eco
    Account Data: When you create a profile to save locations or track your journeys, we collect your chosen username, email address, and minimal demographic preferences.
  • eco
    Usage Data: We monitor how you interact with our maps, articles, and saving features to understand which cultural touchpoints resonate most strongly with our community.
  • eco
    Location Information: With your explicit consent, we access your device's location to suggest nearby heritage sites, hidden ecological trails, and contextually relevant historical data.

3. Data Stewardship & Security

We employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to shield your personal information from unauthorized access. Just as ancient artifacts are protected in climate-controlled environments, your data is housed in secure, modern cloud infrastructure. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

If you have questions about how we handle your data, please reach out to our custodial team.
