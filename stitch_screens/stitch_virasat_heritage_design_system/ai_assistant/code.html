The Western Ghats hold many architectural secrets perfectly integrated with nature. One exceptional site is the Tambdi Surla Temple.

It is a 12th-century Shaivite temple built in the Kadamba style, uniquely carved from black basalt, and remains deeply hidden within the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary.
